# Surface: Chord Changes workbench (index.html)

Mode: Operate. Audience: aspiring and established songwriters, often at the instrument. Job: hear a progression, make it theirs, play or keep it. The redesign replaces the "Writing Desk" look and fixes navigation: tools are hard to find, phone screens feel like hunting, Simple and Advanced feel like two apps.

Keep: chart as a beat-grid timeline, Simple view, light and dark themes, every feature and element ID. Wrong if it looks like a DAW, a toy, a theory textbook or generic SaaS.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The song laid out like a setlist taped to the stage floor: one shell for every view, glanceable from the stool. Refuses the category default of grey cards with a blue button and a different layout per mode.

OWN-WORLD: Stage-black navigation and Play bar framing a white sheet; chords as flat gaffer-tape strips (grey in key, spike blue borrowed, pink secondary, yellow chromatic, violet approach) with one torn end; spike-yellow marks "you are here"; Big Shoulders Display for chord names and headings, Public Sans for UI. Raises: hierarchy by scale contrast alone (specimen); one lettering for a chord everywhere (bitmap); a complete state vocabulary as marks: selected outline, playing green strip, locked pin, outside-limits dashed (reference setting); every view addressable by URL (ticket wallet).

STORY: The visitor sees their chart first, picks what the side panel shows (Write, Chord, Tools) from one nav, and never loses the chart.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: 200px black rail (brand, Write / Chord / Tools, Library, Help, Simple/Advanced at foot), the chart sheet filling the centre with huge tape-strip chords, a 380px panel at right showing the chosen tool, black Play bar along the bottom with a yellow Play. Phone: black top bar, chart, Play bar above a bottom tab bar (Write, Chart, Chord, Tools).

FORM: The Setlist, position 6 of 7 on the grounded list; seed:beb7b2265c8b4994.

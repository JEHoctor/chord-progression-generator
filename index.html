    Harmonize a melody

    One note per beat. The chords follow the chart’s key and time, and .

    Note names like C, F♯ or F#, B♭ or Bb. A dash (-) holds the last note, a dot (.) is a rest, and | between bars is optional. Octaves are ignored; notes are matched by pitch.

    Edit as text
    Write it like a chart: one line per phrase, | between bars, chords sharing a bar split it. Start a line with [Verse] or Chorus: to label a section. Add :2 for an exact beat count, ? for a slot you want ideas for. Ctrl/⌘ + Enter loads. Drag chords in the chart below to reorder them (hold Option/Alt as you drop to copy instead), or drag a chord’s right edge to change its length (hold Shift to trade beats with the next chord); the text updates to match.
